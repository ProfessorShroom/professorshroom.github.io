The first step is taking off the front bumper, I won't cover that here but it's pretty simple. [Here's a guide for removing the front bumper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YRdhzXqk0Rc).
Once the bumper is off remove the blade, DRLs and ballasts, you'll be able to see what's needed to do that once the bumper is off but the blade has one bolt and some clips and the DRLs have two bolts each.
The original 250 grill does need some trimming as the 250 one has some additional plastic that the 265 doesn't have. So you can either replace it with a 265 one, or just trim it which is easy as shown below.

In terms of the loom, if you can get the 265 loom it's pretty much plug and play, you just need to swap two wires around at the main plug connecting to the main loom of the car but you could just cut the plugs off the existing 250 loom and just solder the wires directly onto the new DRLs. It's only 3 wires per DRL. A ground, live and switch so they know to dim when the headlights are on.

You need to swap the yellow and green wires around on the 265 loom to make it work with the 250 as shown above, before and after.

Once that's done it's bascially just the reverse of taking it off, just plug the new DRLs and loom in, bolt it all up to the bumper and away you go.
