After building the [WUPHF.co.uk](/projects/WUPHF/) fan site, I wanted to take it a step further and make a companion app for it, so here's the WUPHF Android app.

It's a fun, fan-made app for fans of The Office, made as a bit of a laugh. It's free on the Google Play Store, so you can grab it below.

### What it does

The app lets you WUPHF friends, family, or anyone else you have an email address for, up to 5 times a day. I added the 5 a day limit so it can't be used to spam people. You know what people are like.

There are also other options like in the show, such as fax, voicemail, X (formerly Twitter) and so on, but those don't actually work and are only there for show.

### Why no iOS version?

I won't be making an iOS version. Apple's developer fees are hard to justify for a fun/fan app that isn't earning anything, so it's Android only for now.
